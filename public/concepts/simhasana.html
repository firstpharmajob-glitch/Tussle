How it opens

Every moving part is in the 3D model above. Press See it at night and they run in this order. Each step locks before the next begins.

  1. SlideThe footstool rides on full-extension ball-bearing slides under the seat, with a soft-close damper and a detent that holds it tucked or docked.
  2. LiftA scissor lift raises the footstool flush with the seat. End stops limit the travel and the pinch points are shielded.
  3. InterlockThe lift works only once the footstool is fully out, so it can never rise while still tucked under the seat.
  4. ReclineThe back pivots on steel hinge pins with a ratchet lock at each of the three angles. Every position holds under load.

Mechanism details are design intent. Ratings, materials and tolerances must be confirmed and tested by the manufacturer before sale.
