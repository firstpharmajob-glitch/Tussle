How the mechanics work

Open See mechanism above to inspect the Blender assembly and scrub its motion.

1. ReleaseTwo concealed latches under the plinth lip free the end sections. No levers or handles show on the top or sides.
2. RiseEach end section pivots up on steel hinge pins, assisted by gas springs so one person can lift it with one hand and it lowers without slamming.
3. UnfoldFoam wedges stored inside the body fold out along the quilted seams. The channels you see by day are the hinge lines.
4. SettleThe middle section drops on a scissor link to form the low part of the wave, with end stops limiting travel and shielded pinch points.
5. LockA ratchet and a positive latch hold each section in place. The wave will not fold under load, and the bench will not open on its own.
6. StoreA push-to-open drawer hides in the plinth on soft-close slides, with room for a towel and wipes. Its front matches the base, so it reads as trim.

Mechanism details are design intent. Ratings, materials and tolerances must be confirmed and tested by the manufacturer before sale.
