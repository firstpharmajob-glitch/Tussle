How it works: unlock, the back lifts and lays backward to horizontal, then glides forward over the seat and sits on it. It matches the seat size (199.4 × 58 cm), so it stacks as a double cushion.

Concept model with placeholder dimensions. Rails, locks and load ratings need engineering review and real testing before production.

Before production
  • Rails and locks: lock in both positions, no drift, no noise, no finger pinch points at rails and hinge.
  • Arms and frame: strong, comfortable to grip. Legs lock against slipping.
  • Moving (dynamic) load test, not only static. Stability, fire-retardant cover and load tests to the sofa standards, with independent testing.
  • Wipeable cover must be tested as body-safe before use.
