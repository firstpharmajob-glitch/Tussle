How the mechanics work

The whole sequence is visible in the 3D model above. Drag the Top slider to step through it.

1. LiftGas-spring-assisted lift rails raise the top 30 cm clear of the frame, so the turn has room and the legs and rails stay out of the way.
2. TurnThe top rotates 180° about its long axis on steel pivot pins. Guards cover the pinch points at each end.
3. LowerThe top lowers back into the frame on a soft-close damper, with end stops and no slam.
4. LockA positive latch at each end locks the top in both positions. It cannot lift or turn under load.
5. StoreThe shelf stays open and stable the whole time, below the moving parts.
6. A simpler versionA lift-off reversible top with no mechanism. Less to engineer, a little more effort each night.

The flip mechanism (lift, rotate, lower) needs engineering review for pinch points, locks and load rating before production.
